Plumbit ERP connects sales, purchases, inventory, accounting, and CRM in one tenant. Operational documents carry business data; posting finalizes them and updates stock and/or the general ledger.

**Master data feeds every transaction.** Customers and suppliers (parties), products, warehouses, taxes, and price lists are referenced when you create quotations, orders, receipts, and invoices. Without them, compose screens cannot build valid lines.

**Sales and purchases drive inventory.** Stock on hand changes when you **post** goods receipts, delivery notes, returns, adjustments, and transfers — not when you save a purchase order or confirm a sales order. Sales orders **reserve** available quantity on confirm; delivery notes **issue** stock and post COGS.

**All three feed accounting.** Posted delivery notes post COGS. Posted goods receipts post inventory and GRNI. Posted sales and purchase invoices post AR/AP, revenue/purchases, and VAT. Payments first hit advance accounts, then allocate to open invoices.

**CRM sits upstream of sales.** Leads convert to customers and opportunities. Quotations can link to an opportunity; there is no automatic quote-from-opportunity conversion.
