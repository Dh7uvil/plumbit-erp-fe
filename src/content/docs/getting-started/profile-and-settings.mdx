Personal settings are under the **Settings** sidebar group. All authenticated users can access these screens regardless of module permissions.

## Routes

| Screen | Route | What you can change |
|---|---|---|
| My profile | <AppLink href="/settings/profile">/settings/profile</AppLink> | Name, phone |
| Change password | <AppLink href="/settings/password">/settings/password</AppLink> | Password (signs out other sessions) |
| Notification settings | <AppLink href="/settings/notifications">/settings/notifications</AppLink> | Email, in-app, WhatsApp toggles |

`/settings` redirects to profile.

## Profile fields

**Editable:** display name, phone number.

**Read-only:** email, account status, assigned roles, last login, employee code, designation.

Organization-wide settings (branches, warehouses, credit policy, VAT) live in <AppLink href="/organization-settings">Organization settings</AppLink> — see <DocLink href="/docs/organization-and-access/organization-settings">Organization settings</DocLink>.

## Password change

After a successful password change, all other active sessions are invalidated. You remain signed in on the current device.
