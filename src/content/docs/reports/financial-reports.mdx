Financial reports summarize the general ledger, books of account, and statutory financial statements. All require posted journal data.

## Typical filters

Date range (from/to) or as-of date, branch, cost center where applicable. Trial balance and financial statements respect **period lock** — see <DocLink href="/docs/accounting/period-lock">Period lock</DocLink>.

## Using trial balance

Start here for month-end: verify debits equal credits, drill to general ledger for any account, reconcile control accounts (AR, AP, inventory, VAT) to sub-ledger reports.

GRNI balance appears on the `GOODS_RECEIVED_NOT_INVOICED` system account — there is no separate GRNI aging report. See <DocLink href="/docs/getting-started/implementation-notes">Implementation notes</DocLink>.
