Sales and purchase analysis reports break down **posted** revenue and purchase spend by dimensions such as customer, supplier, product, and period.

<AppLink href="/reports/sales-analysis">Sales analysis</AppLink> · <AppLink href="/reports/purchase-analysis">Purchase analysis</AppLink>

## Sales analysis

Route: <AppLink href="/reports/sales-analysis">/reports/sales-analysis</AppLink>

Summarizes posted sales invoice lines:

| Typical breakdown | Use |
|---|---|
| By customer | Top customers, concentration |
| By product / category | Product mix |
| By period | Trend over months |
| By branch / salesperson | Territory performance |

Includes net amounts and quantities from **posted** invoices only. Credit notes reduce revenue in the period posted.

## Purchase analysis

Route: <AppLink href="/reports/purchase-analysis">/reports/purchase-analysis</AppLink>

Summarizes posted purchase invoice lines:

| Typical breakdown | Use |
|---|---|
| By supplier | Spend concentration |
| By product / category | Procurement mix |
| By period | Spend trend |
| By expense category | Freight, duty, goods split |

Includes **posted** goods and expense bills. Debit notes adjust in the period posted.

## Filters

Date range, branch, party, product, and category filters where supported. Export CSV with `reports.report.export`.

Permission: `reports.report.financial` (grouped under Financial reports in the hub).
