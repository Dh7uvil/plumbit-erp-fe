Inventory reports show stock quantities, FIFO valuation, movement history, and reorder-driven purchase suggestions.

<AppLink href="/reports">Reports hub</AppLink>

## Reports

| Report | Route | What it shows |
|---|---|---|
| Stock valuation | <AppLink href="/reports/stock-valuation">/reports/stock-valuation</AppLink> | On-hand qty and value at FIFO landed cost by product/warehouse |
| Stock valuation vs GL | <AppLink href="/reports/stock-valuation-gl">/reports/stock-valuation-gl</AppLink> | Inventory sub-ledger total vs GL inventory control account |
| Stock movement | <AppLink href="/reports/stock-movement">/reports/stock-movement</AppLink> | Receipts, issues, adjustments, transfers for a period |
| Stock aging | <AppLink href="/reports/stock-aging">/reports/stock-aging</AppLink> | Age of FIFO layers by receipt date |
| Purchase suggestions | <AppLink href="/reports/purchase-suggestions">/reports/purchase-suggestions</AppLink> | Products at or below reorder level with suggested qty |

Permission: `reports.report.inventory`.

## Data rules

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Valuation method | FIFO landed unit cost from posted receipts and landed costs |
| Draft exclusion | Draft GRNs, DNs, adjustments do not appear |
| Available qty | On hand − reserved − QC hold — see <DocLink href="/docs/inventory/stock-concepts">Stock concepts</DocLink> |

Dashboard **Stock valuation** KPI links to the valuation report.

## Typical use

| Scenario | Report |
|---|---|
| Balance sheet inventory | Stock valuation → compare to stock valuation vs GL |
| Slow-moving stock | Stock aging |
| Audit trail | Stock movement for date range |
| Replenishment review | Purchase suggestions — see <DocLink href="/docs/planning-and-ai/stock-recommendations">Stock recommendations</DocLink> |
