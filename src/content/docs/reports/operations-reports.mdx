## Reports

| Report | Route | What it shows |
|---|---|---|
| Three-way match | <AppLink href="/reports/three-way-match">/reports/three-way-match</AppLink> | PO line ordered, received, and billed qty/value comparison |
| Received not billed | <AppLink href="/reports/received-not-billed">/reports/received-not-billed</AppLink> | Posted GRN lines with remaining unbilled quantity |

Permission: `reports.report.inventory`.

## Three-way match

Informational only — variances do **not** block GRN or bill posting. Full detail: <DocLink href="/docs/purchases/three-way-match">Three-way match</DocLink>.

Match statuses per PO line:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| UNRECEIVED | Nothing received yet |
| UNBILLED | Received but not billed |
| QTY_VARIANCE | Quantity mismatch across order/receipt/bill |
| PRICE_VARIANCE | Quantities align but unit price differs |
| MATCHED | Fully reconciled |

Only **posted** bills count toward billed figures.

## Received not billed

Shows GRNI exposure at the line level — goods in stock but not yet on a supplier bill. Use alongside trial balance GRNI control account for month-end AP review.
