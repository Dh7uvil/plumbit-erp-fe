Receivables and payables reports show **open balances** and **aging** from posted sales invoices, bills, opening balances, and unapplied payments.

### Legacy document statements

Customer statement, supplier statement, and account statement **with a party but no GL account** all use the same legacy document model. **Cost sheets and vouchers** on party statements follow posted journals: one row per **party journal line** (net per line), with document-level voucher fallback when no party line exists. Account statement **with both a party and an account** stays on posted journal lines (GL drill-down with party filter): **PO** / **SO** for issued orders, **PI** / **SI** only when not linked to an order, plus **OB**, **WO**, **SR** / **RR**, payments, notes, cost sheets, and vouchers.

Filters: **include opening** (previous balance), **include PDC** (pending cheques).

**Reconciliation:** Document statement closing vs AR/AP aging vs GL control account — order lines replace linked posted invoices on the statement. Use account statement with the AR/AP **account** selected (optionally with a party filter) for journal-level GL tie-out, including manual journals and allocations.

### Account statement (GL account selected)

When you pick a **ledger account** (with or without a party filter), the report uses **legacy statement-of-account** rules on posted journals. Lines are **cost sheets** (CSI/CSE/CSO ref and type), **vouchers** from the vouchers module (CP/CR/BP/BR/CV/GP/JV type codes with **CPV/CRV/…** refs — not the GL **JV** series), payments, and related documents — not manual journal entries or storno reversal rows. Cost sheet and voucher posting reuse the **document number** on the GL entry (CSI…, CPV…, etc.). Use <AppLink href="/reports/general-ledger">General ledger</AppLink> for manual journals.

- **Cost sheets and vouchers** (journal, cash/bank receipt/payment, contra, payment voucher): one row per **ledger line** on the selected account, with the same **debit/credit columns** as on the document; only the **current** cost sheet journal counts (stale repost orphans are ignored). Repost/reversal cycles net **per line slot**. Linked **reversal/storno journals** are for netting only — not separate rows. **Cheque No** shows cheque metadata only (not cost sheet or voucher numbers).
- **Other documents** (payments, bills, notes): one net row per document when reposted/reversed.
- **Supplier-style columns** for cost sheets (**credit**), supplier payments (**debit**), and supplier journal vouchers — same as the supplier statement, including when the selected account is an expense/freight ledger touched by the cost sheet.
- **Previous balance** shows the brought-forward **balance** only (not repeated period debits/credits).
- **P.D.C** lines are omitted when the cheque issue journal is already on the statement.

For **every journal line** on an account (audit / GL detail), use <AppLink href="/reports/general-ledger">General ledger</AppLink> or cash/bank/day books — those reports intentionally stay at line level.

## What is included

| Component | Source |
|---|---|
| Open invoice balances | Posted sales/bills minus allocations |
| Opening AR/AP | Opening balance items |
| Unapplied receipts/payments | Reduce exposure where not yet allocated |

Amounts respect invoice currency with base-currency totals where shown. See <DocLink href="/docs/accounting/accounts-receivable">Accounts receivable</DocLink> and <DocLink href="/docs/accounting/accounts-payable">Accounts payable</DocLink>.

## Typical use

| Scenario | Report |
|---|---|
| Collections priority | AR aging — filter overdue bucket |
| Supplier payment run | AP aging or outstanding bills |
| Customer query | Customer statement for date range |
| Month-end reconciliation | AR/AP aging vs trial balance control accounts |

Dashboard **Open AR** and **Open AP** KPIs link directly to aging reports. Only **posted** documents count.
