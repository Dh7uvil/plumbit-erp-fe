Receivables and payables reports show **open balances** and **aging** from posted sales invoices, bills, opening balances, and unapplied payments.

<AppLink href="/reports">Reports hub</AppLink>

## Reports

| Report | Route | What it shows |
|---|---|---|
| AR aging | <AppLink href="/reports/ar-aging">/reports/ar-aging</AppLink> | Customer balances by age bucket (current, 30, 60, 90+) |
| AP aging | <AppLink href="/reports/ap-aging">/reports/ap-aging</AppLink> | Supplier balances by age bucket |
| Outstanding invoices | <AppLink href="/reports/outstanding-invoices">/reports/outstanding-invoices</AppLink> | Open sales invoices with balance due |
| Outstanding bills | <AppLink href="/reports/outstanding-bills">/reports/outstanding-bills</AppLink> | Open bills with balance due |
| Customer statement | <AppLink href="/reports/customer-statement">/reports/customer-statement</AppLink> | Running AR statement for one customer |
| Supplier statement | <AppLink href="/reports/supplier-statement">/reports/supplier-statement</AppLink> | Running AP statement for one supplier |

Permission: `reports.report.ar_ap`.

## What is included

| Component | Source |
|---|---|
| Open invoice balances | Posted sales/bills minus allocations |
| Opening AR/AP | Opening balance items |
| Unapplied receipts/payments | Reduce exposure where not yet allocated |

Amounts respect invoice currency with base-currency totals where shown. See <DocLink href="/docs/accounting/accounts-receivable">Accounts receivable</DocLink> and <DocLink href="/docs/accounting/accounts-payable">Accounts payable</DocLink>.

## Typical use

| Scenario | Report |
|---|---|
| Collections priority | AR aging — filter overdue bucket |
| Supplier payment run | AP aging or outstanding bills |
| Customer query | Customer statement for date range |
| Month-end reconciliation | AR/AP aging vs trial balance control accounts |

Dashboard **Open AR** and **Open AP** KPIs link directly to aging reports. Only **posted** documents count.
