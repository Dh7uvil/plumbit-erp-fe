CRM reports analyze pipeline health, conversion, and sales team activity. They complement operational sales reports which focus on posted invoices.

<AppLink href="/reports">Reports hub</AppLink>

## Reports

| Report | Route | What it shows |
|---|---|---|
| Sales pipeline | <AppLink href="/reports/sales-pipeline">/reports/sales-pipeline</AppLink> | Open opportunities by stage with value |
| Sales funnel | <AppLink href="/reports/sales-funnel">/reports/sales-funnel</AppLink> | Stage-to-stage conversion counts |
| Win / loss | <AppLink href="/reports/win-loss">/reports/win-loss</AppLink> | Won vs lost deals with lost reasons |
| Lead conversion | <AppLink href="/reports/lead-conversion">/reports/lead-conversion</AppLink> | Lead volume and conversion rate by source |
| Sales activity | <AppLink href="/reports/sales-activity">/reports/sales-activity</AppLink> | Completed activities by owner and type |

Permission: `reports.report.crm`. CSV export requires `reports.report.export`.

## Dashboard links

| Dashboard KPI | Report |
|---|---|
| Open pipeline | Sales pipeline |
| Closing this month | Opportunities list (not a report) |
| Overdue activities | Activities list with `view=overdue` |

## Data scope

| Includes | Excludes |
|---|---|
| Open and closed opportunities | Draft quotations |
| Lead status history | Posted invoice revenue (use sales analysis) |
| Completed and open activities | Stock or AR balances |

Pipeline value uses opportunity **expected value** in opportunity currency.
