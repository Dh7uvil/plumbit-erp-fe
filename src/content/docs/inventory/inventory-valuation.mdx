Plumbit ERP values inventory using **FIFO** (first-in, first-out). This page is the canonical reference for costing — other docs link here rather than repeating layer logic.

<AppLink href="/reports/stock-valuation">Open stock valuation report</AppLink>

## Cost layers

Each inbound movement that affects costing creates a **StockCostLayer** per product × warehouse:

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `qty_received` | Original inbound quantity |
| `qty_remaining` | Unconsumed quantity |
| `landed_unit_cost` | Unit cost including allocated landed costs |
| `document_date` | Layer receipt date (FIFO ordering) |

View layers from the stock detail screen for any balance.

## Inbound — creating layers

Layers are created on post for: goods receipt, opening stock, found/adjustment increases, sales return restock, transfer in (carried from transfer out consumptions), and similar inbound movement types listed in <DocLink href="/docs/inventory/how-stock-changes">How stock changes</DocLink>.

Initial unit cost comes from the document line (GRN rate, adjustment unit cost, etc.).

## Outbound — consuming layers

On outbound post (delivery note, transfer out, damage, etc.) the costing service consumes **oldest layers first** by `document_date`, then layer id.

The movement stores:

- **`landed_unit_cost`** on the movement row = weighted average of consumed layers (`total value / qty`)
- **`value`** = total FIFO cost of the issue (negative for outbound)

If layers are insufficient and `allow_negative_stock` is enabled, a synthetic layer at fallback cost (product purchase rate) may be created.

## Revaluation

`landed_unit_cost` on remaining layers can increase when:

- **Landed cost vouchers** allocate freight/duty/charges to GRN layers
- **Purchase invoice** posts a different unit cost than the GRN (PPV revalue)

Revaluation updates layer cost and posts GL (Dr Inventory, Cr charge clearing or GRNI variance) — it does not change on-hand quantity.

## Valuation report vs GL

<AppLink href="/reports/stock-valuation">Stock valuation</AppLink> sums `qty_remaining × landed_unit_cost` across all layers as of a date.

The **GL reconciliation** view on the same report compares:

| Measure | Source |
|---|---|
| **Valuation total** | Sum of FIFO layers |
| **GL balance** | Inventory system-role account balance as of date |
| **Difference** | Should be zero when all stock-affecting documents are posted |

<Callout type="note">
Stock transfers are GL-neutral — they move cost between warehouse layers but do not change total inventory GL. GRNI timing (receipt before invoice) can temporarily skew GL vs layers until the purchase invoice clears GRNI.
</Callout>
