A stock transfer moves quantity from one warehouse to another in a **single post** — there is no in-transit step or separate receive document.

## What post does

For each line:

1. **TRANSFER_OUT** at the source warehouse — on hand decreases; FIFO layers are consumed oldest-first.
2. **TRANSFER_IN** at the destination warehouse — on hand increases; the **same cost consumptions** are re-applied as new layers (cost is carried, not re-averaged).

Transfers are **GL-neutral** — inventory stays on one inventory account; only warehouse balances change.

## Requirements

- Source and destination warehouses must differ.
- Outbound quantity is validated against **available** (on hand − reserved − QC hold) unless `allow_negative_stock` is enabled — see <DocLink href="/docs/inventory/negative-stock-and-shortfalls">Negative stock and shortfalls</DocLink>.
