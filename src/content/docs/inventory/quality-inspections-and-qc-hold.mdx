Products flagged **QC required** put received quantity on **QC hold** at goods receipt post. Goods stay in on-hand but are not **available** until a quality inspection is approved.

## Lifecycle

<LifecycleDiagram
  mainPath={["DRAFT", "APPROVED"]}

    { from: "DRAFT", action: "cancel", to: "CANCELLED" },
  ]}
/>

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **DRAFT** | Quantities and dispositions editable; linked GRN must be posted |
| **APPROVED** | Hold released or stock written off per line disposition |
| **CANCELLED** | Voided draft — no stock effect |

Approved inspections **cannot be reversed**.

## Line quantities

Each line must satisfy: `qty_accepted + qty_rejected + qty_rework = qty_inspected`, and inspected qty cannot exceed remaining hold on the GRN line.

## Dispositions on approve

| Outcome | Stock effect | GL |
|---|---|---|
| **Accepted** | QC hold − (`QC_RELEASE` audit) | None |
| **Rejected → RETURN_TO_SUPPLIER** | Stays on QC hold | None — purchase return posts the stock-out |
| **Rejected → SCRAP** | On hand −, hold − (`DAMAGE` movement) | Dr Stock scrap, Cr Inventory |
| **Rework → REWORK_RELEASE** | QC hold − for rework qty (`QC_RELEASE`) | None |

<Callout type="note">
`QC_HOLD` / `QC_RELEASE` rows are audit-only — on-hand and FIFO layers are unchanged. Only scrap and purchase return remove rejected quantity from stock.
</Callout>

## Auto-creation

Posting a GRN with QC-required lines creates a draft inspection pre-filled with hold quantities (accepted = full hold, rejected = zero). Edit before approving.
