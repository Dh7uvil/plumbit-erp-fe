Inbound stock enters through **goods receipts** (GRNs). Posting a GRN is the only standard way to receive purchase stock into a warehouse.

## What happens on GRN post

- **On hand** increases at the GRN header warehouse (default warehouse if unset).
- A **FIFO cost layer** is created at the line unit cost.
- GL: **Dr Inventory, Cr GRNI**.
- If the product requires QC, quantity also goes on **QC hold** — available stays unchanged until inspection.

PO **issue** only raises **incoming**; it does not receive stock. See <DocLink href="/docs/inventory/how-stock-changes">How stock changes</DocLink>.
