Stock is tracked **per product per warehouse** in a `StockBalance` record. This page is the canonical explanation of quantity fields and the available formula.

## Quantity fields

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **On hand** (`qty_on_hand`) | Physical quantity in the warehouse |
| **Reserved** (`qty_reserved`) | Soft hold for confirmed sales orders — not a movement |
| **QC hold** (`qty_quality_hold`) | Received but not yet released by quality inspection — still in on hand |
| **Incoming** (`qty_incoming`) | Expected from issued purchase orders — not yet received |
| **Available** | `on hand − reserved − QC hold` — what you can sell or deliver |

<Callout type="note">
QC hold reduces **available** but not **on hand**. Goods on QC hold are physically in the warehouse but not sellable.
</Callout>

## What does not use these fields

- **Outgoing** and **in transit** columns exist on the balance model but are **not updated** by any current transaction. Stock transfers move quantity immediately in one step.

## Reorder settings

**Reorder level** and **reorder quantity** are set per product **per warehouse** on the stock balance — not on the product master. The purchase suggestions report uses available qty vs reorder level.

## Service and non-tracked products

Products with `item_type = SERVICE` or `track_inventory = false` skip stock operations. They can appear on orders and invoices but never create movements or reservations.
