Accounts receivable (AR) is the **open balance customers owe** after posted sales invoices, opening AR items, credit notes, and customer payments. AR is tracked as **open items** in document currency with base amounts for reporting.

## Open items

Each posted receivable creates an **open item** until fully settled:

| Item type | Source |
|---|---|
| `SALES_INVOICE` | Posted sales invoice |
| `OPENING_AR` | Opening balances wizard |
| `CREDIT_NOTE` | Unapplied credit balance |
| `CUSTOMER_PAYMENT` | Unapplied receipt balance |

Party-specific AR accounts on customers override the system **ACCOUNTS_RECEIVABLE** role when set.

## Aging buckets

<AppLink href="/reports/ar-aging">AR aging</AppLink> groups outstanding items by days past due (or document date when no due date):

| Bucket | Days past due |
|---|---|
| Current | 0 or not yet due |
| 1–30 | 1–30 |
| 31–60 | 31–60 |
| 61–90 | 61–90 |
| 91+ | Over 90 |
| Unapplied credits | Credit notes and unallocated payments |

Items dated after the **as-of** date are excluded. Items without a resolvable base amount are skipped with a warning.

## Customer statement

<AppLink href="/reports/customer-statement">Customer statement</AppLink> lists opening balance, invoices, payments, credits, and running balance for one customer over a date range — sourced from open items and posted journals with party tags.

## GL linkage

Posting patterns (Dr AR, Cr revenue/VAT) are in <DocLink href="/docs/accounting/accounting-overview">Accounting overview</DocLink>. AR subledger totals should reconcile to the AR GL account balance.
