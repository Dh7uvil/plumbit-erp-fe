Cheques and post-dated cheques (PDC) track **inbound customer cheques** and **outbound supplier cheques** through issue, deposit, clear, or bounce — separate from the customer/supplier payment screens.

## GL on issue

| Direction | Debit | Credit |
|---|---|---|
| **Inbound** (customer) | CHEQUES_RECEIVABLE | Advance from customer |
| **Outbound** (supplier) | Advance to supplier | CHEQUES_PAYABLE |

On **issue**, optional allocations apply the advance to open AR/AP items (same pattern as payments).

## GL on clear

| Direction | Debit | Credit |
|---|---|---|
| **Inbound** | Bank | CHEQUES_RECEIVABLE |
| **Outbound** | CHEQUES_PAYABLE | Bank |

Clear date uses `due_date` when set, otherwise `cheque_date`.

## Bounce reverses

Bouncing reverses in order:

1. **Clearing journal** (if cheque was cleared)
2. **Allocation journals** (if allocations were applied)
3. **Issue journal**

Status returns to **BOUNCED**; unapplied amount resets to full cheque amount.

<Callout type="note">
Inbound cheques can skip **DEPOSITED** and go **ISSUED → CLEARED** directly. Deposit is tracking-only with no additional GL entry.
</Callout>
