Recurring templates **generate draft sales invoices or purchase bills** on a schedule. Generation never posts — a user must review and post each draft like any other document.

<AppLink href="/recurring">Open recurring templates</AppLink>

## How generation works

| Step | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| Schedule | `next_run_date` advanced by frequency (monthly, quarterly, etc.) |
| Worker | Due active templates enqueue generation events |
| Output | **DRAFT** sales invoice or bill copied from template lines |
| Idempotency | One draft per template per run date — duplicates rejected |

Templates with status **ACTIVE** generate on or after `next_run_date`. Completed templates (past end date) move to **COMPLETED**.

<Callout type="important">
Recurring generation **creates DRAFT only**. No GL, stock, or open items until manual post. Listed under "No GL" in <DocLink href="/docs/accounting/accounting-overview">Accounting overview</DocLink>.
</Callout>

## Template contents

Templates store party, currency, lines (products/expenses), tax treatment, and branch — same fields as the target document type. Generated drafts include a `[recurring:{template_id}:{run_date}]` trace in narration.
