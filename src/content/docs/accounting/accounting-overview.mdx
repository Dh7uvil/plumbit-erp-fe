Accounting in Plumbit ERP is driven by **posted operational documents** and manual journals. `LedgerPostingService` is the sole writer of journal entries.

## How accounts are resolved

GL accounts are mapped via **system roles** (`AccountSystemRole`) on the chart of accounts — not org-setting default account IDs. Party-specific AR/AP overrides on customers and suppliers take precedence over system AR/AP.

## Document → journal summary

| Document (on post) | Debit | Credit |
|---|---|---|
| Goods receipt | Inventory | GRNI |
| Delivery note | COGS | Inventory |
| Sales invoice | AR | Revenue, VAT output, shipping/charges |
| Purchase invoice (with GRN) | GRNI (+ inventory/PPV variance) | AP (+ VAT input) |
| Purchase invoice (no GRN, product) | Purchases | AP |
| Purchase return | GRNI | Inventory |
| Sales return (restock) | Inventory | COGS |
| Sales return (scrap) | Stock scrap | Inventory |
| QC scrap | Stock scrap | Inventory |
| Stock adjustment (+) | Inventory | Inventory adjustment |
| Stock adjustment (−) | Inventory adjustment | Inventory |
| Credit note | Sales returns, VAT | AR |
| Debit note | AP | GRNI or expense, VAT |
| Customer payment | Bank | Advance from customer |
| Payment allocation | Advance | AR |
| Supplier payment | Advance to supplier | Bank |
| Supplier allocation | AP | Advance |
| Landed cost | Inventory (+ variance) | Charge clearing |
| Write-off (SI) | Bad debt expense | AR |
| FX revaluation | Control / FX gain-loss | Opposite |
| Opening balances | As entered | Equity plug + AP |

**No GL:** stock transfers, packages, shipments, cost sheets, budgets, recurring invoice generation (drafts only).

## Reversal model

Cancelling a posted document creates a **reversal journal** (`JournalType.REVERSAL`). The original entry remains `POSTED` with `reversed_by_id` set. Manual posted journals are **reversed**, not deleted.

## Books start

System journals require `books_start_date` on the tenant unless the user has period override. The opening balances wizard sets this date and hard-locks prior periods.
