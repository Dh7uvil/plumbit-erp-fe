Bank reconciliation matches **imported bank statement lines** to **posted book entries** on the linked bank GL account. Unmatched items explain the gap between the bank closing balance and your ledger.

## Import statement

Create a statement for a bank account and period, then import lines (date, reference, debit/credit). Each line starts as **UNMATCHED**.

Book entries are drawn from posted journal lines hitting the bank account's GL account within the statement date range.

## Suggested match scoring

The system scores unmatched statement lines against unmatched book entries:

| Signal | Points |
|---|---|
| Amount equal | +50 |
| Date equal | +30 |
| Reference equal (case-insensitive) | +20 |

Only pairs scoring **≥ 50** appear as suggestions. Results sort by score descending. A perfect amount+date+ref match scores **100**.

## Manual match

Accept a suggestion or manually link a statement line to a journal line. Lines can also be **excluded** when they should not affect reconciliation (e.g. bank-only fees not yet booked).

<Callout type="note">
Matching is one-to-one. Already-matched book entries are excluded from further suggestions.
</Callout>

## Reconcile

Reconciliation completes only when every statement line is **matched or excluded**. The reconciliation statement shows:

- Bank closing balance (from import)
- Unmatched statement total
- Unmatched book total
- **Reconciled balance** = closing − unmatched statement + unmatched book
