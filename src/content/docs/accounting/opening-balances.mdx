The opening balances wizard is the **go-live commit** for accounting and inventory. One transaction posts GL balances, AR/AP open items, opening stock, sets `books_start_date`, and applies a **hard period lock** on all prior dates.

<AppLink href="/opening-balances">Open opening balances</AppLink>

## Wizard inputs

| Section | Creates |
|---|---|
| **GL lines** | Direct debit/credit per account |
| **AR items** | Dr AR per customer with due date |
| **AP items** | Cr AP per supplier with due date |
| **Stock lines** | Dr inventory via `OPENING_STOCK` movements |

Entry date = `books_start_date − 1 day`. Journal type = **OPENING_BALANCE**.

## Equity plug

Debits and credits must balance. Any difference posts automatically to **OPENING_BALANCE_EQUITY**:

- Net debit excess → Cr opening balance equity
- Net credit excess → Dr opening balance equity

Preview shows the plug line before commit.

## Stock lines

Each stock line creates a **OPENING_STOCK** movement at `unit_cost × quantity`. Total layer value must equal the inventory GL debit or commit fails with a mismatch error.

## Hard lock

On commit:

- `books_start_date` is saved on the tenant
- `hard_lock_date` = entry date (day before books start)
- Reason: "Opening balances committed"

No document dated on or before the hard lock can post — even with period override.

<Callout type="note">
Reset is allowed only when **no other posted activity** exists after go-live. Reset reverses the opening journal and stock movements.
</Callout>

<Steps>
<Step title="Preview">
Enter GL, AR/AP, and stock lines. Review equity plug and totals.
</Step>
<Step title="Commit">
Single idempotent post — cannot commit twice.
</Step>
<Step title="Operate">
All system journals require `books_start_date`. Begin normal posting from books start date forward.
</Step>
</Steps>
