Bank accounts are **operational records** that link a real bank account (name, IBAN, currency) to a **postable GL account** on the chart. Payments, vouchers, cheques, and bank reconciliation all resolve cash movement through this link.

<AppLink href="/bank-accounts">Open bank accounts</AppLink>

## GL link requirement

Each bank account must reference a chart account with:

- `account_subtype = BANK`
- `is_group = false` (postable)
- Typically mapped to system role **BANK** (or a dedicated bank ledger account under Current Assets)

<Callout type="note">
Creating a bank account without a valid BANK chart account is rejected. Set up the chart first — see <DocLink href="/docs/accounting/chart-of-accounts">Chart of accounts</DocLink>.
</Callout>

## What uses bank accounts

| Feature | Role |
|---|---|
| Customer / supplier payments | Receipt or disbursement bank |
| Vouchers (BRV/BPV) | Bank leg of receipt/payment |
| Cheques (clear) | Dr/Cr bank on clear |
| Bank reconciliation | Book side matched to statement lines |
| FX revaluation | Foreign-currency bank balances revalued at period end |

## Fields

| Field | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `account_id` | Linked BANK GL account |
| `currency_id` | Bank account currency (may differ from base) |
| `is_default` | Default selection on payment screens |
| IBAN / account number | Reference only — no GL impact |

<Steps>
<Step title="Create BANK chart account">
Under Current Assets, add or use a postable account with subtype **BANK**. Map system role **BANK** if this is the primary bank ledger.
</Step>
<Step title="Register bank account">
<AppLink href="/bank-accounts">Bank accounts</AppLink> → link to the chart account, set currency and details.
</Step>
<Step title="Use on transactions">
Select on customer payments, supplier payments, vouchers, and cheques. Reconcile via <DocLink href="/docs/accounting/bank-reconciliation">Bank reconciliation</DocLink>.
</Step>
</Steps>
