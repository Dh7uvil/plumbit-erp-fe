Write-offs remove **uncollectible AR** (or unpayable AP) from open balances without a cash receipt. On sales invoices, the action posts Dr bad debt expense, Cr AR and blocks invoice cancellation.

## Sales invoice write-off

Available on **posted** sales invoices with `balance_due > 0`.

    { action: "Reverse write-off", stock: "No change", reserved: "No change", gl: "Reversal journal", tax: "None", documents: "`amount_written_off` decreases" },
    { action: "Cancel posted SI", stock: "No change", reserved: "No change", gl: "—", tax: "—", documents: "**Blocked** if `amount_written_off > 0`" },

Amount must be positive and ≤ `balance_due`. Default expense account resolves from **BAD_DEBT_EXPENSE** system role (override allowed).

<Callout type="important">
Once written off, cancel the sales invoice only after **reversing the write-off**. Same guard applies when `amount_paid > 0` or `amount_credited > 0`.
</Callout>

## Bill write-off

Purchase bills support write-off with Dr AP, Cr expense — for supplier balances that will not be paid. Same reversal pattern; blocks cancellation while write-off amount remains.
