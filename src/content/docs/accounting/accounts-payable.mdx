Accounts payable (AP) is the **open balance owed to suppliers** after posted bills, opening AP items, debit notes, and supplier payments. Like AR, AP is tracked as **open items** in document currency.

## Open items

| Item type | Source |
|---|---|
| `PURCHASE_INVOICE` | Posted bill |
| `OPENING_AP` | Opening balances wizard |
| `DEBIT_NOTE` | Unapplied debit balance |
| `SUPPLIER_PAYMENT` | Unapplied payment balance |

Party-specific AP accounts on suppliers override the system **ACCOUNTS_PAYABLE** role when set.

## Aging buckets

<AppLink href="/reports/ap-aging">AP aging</AppLink> uses the same bucket logic as AR:

| Bucket | Days past due |
|---|---|
| Current | 0 or not yet due |
| 1–30 | 1–30 |
| 31–60 | 31–60 |
| 61–90 | 61–90 |
| 91+ | Over 90 |
| Unapplied credits | Debit notes and unallocated payments |

## Supplier statement

<AppLink href="/reports/supplier-statement">Supplier statement</AppLink> shows bills, payments, debits, and running balance for one supplier over a date range.

**Supplier statement** and **account statement (with a supplier selected)** use legacy document lines: issued/closed **purchase orders** as credit (**PO**), posted bills **without** a PO as **PI**, plus opening AP, payments, debits, write-offs, refunds, cost sheets, and vouchers. PO-linked posted bills are omitted so the PO is not double-counted.

These document statements can differ from <AppLink href="/reports/ap-aging">AP aging</AppLink> and the AP GL control account. Reconcile with the GL **account statement** (journal lines on the AP account, no party filter) or trial balance when you need subledger tie-out.
