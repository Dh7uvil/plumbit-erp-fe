Vouchers record **cash and bank receipts, payments, and contra transfers** with optional allocation to open invoices. They follow the same DRAFT → POSTED lifecycle as other financial documents.

<AppLink href="/vouchers">Open vouchers</AppLink>

## Voucher types

| Type | Prefix | Cash/bank leg | Typical use |
|---|---|---|---|
| **CASH_RECEIPT** | CRV | Cash (CASH subtype) | Petty cash received |
| **CASH_PAYMENT** | CPV | Cash | Petty cash paid |
| **BANK_RECEIPT** | BRV | Bank (BANK subtype) | Bank deposit / receipt |
| **BANK_PAYMENT** | BPV | Bank | Bank payment |
| **CONTRA** | CON | Two cash/bank accounts | Transfer between accounts |

Receipt vouchers (CRV, BRV) credit **Advance from customer** or debit AR on allocation. Payment vouchers (CPV, BPV) debit **Advance to supplier** or credit AP on allocation. **CONTRA** moves between cash/bank accounts with no party allocation.
