Product categories organize your catalog and provide default GL accounts for revenue and purchases.

<AppLink href="/categories">Open categories</AppLink>

## What it controls

| Field | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `code` | Unique identifier |
| `name` | Category label |
| `parent_id` | Optional hierarchy (parent category) |
| `income_account_id` | Default revenue account for products in this category |
| `purchase_account_id` | Default purchase/expense account |
| `is_active` | Inactive categories cannot be assigned |

## Downstream fields

| Record | Field / behavior |
|---|---|
| Product | `category_id` |
| GL posting | If product lacks income/purchase accounts, category accounts apply, then system defaults (`SALES_REVENUE`, `PURCHASES`) |

Account resolve order on products is documented in <DocLink href="/docs/master-data/products">Products</DocLink>.

## Where used

Product master, product list filters, and import/export. Categories do not appear directly on document lines — the product carries the category.

## Constraints

- Unique `(tenant_id, code)` among active rows.
- Parent cycle detection on update.

## Example

Category "Electronics" sets income account 4100 and purchase account 5100. A new product in Electronics inherits those accounts unless overridden at product level.
