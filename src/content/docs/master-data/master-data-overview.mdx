## What is seeded automatically

New tenants receive: ISO currencies (AED base), four VAT taxes, units (PCS, BOX, M, KG), a `MAIN` warehouse, Net 30 payment terms, standard terms template, document sequences, 16 charge types, UAE chart of accounts, and CRM foundations.

## Before first transaction

| Check | Why |
|---|---|
| Products with correct `track_inventory` and accounts | Stock and GL posting depend on product setup |
| Customers/suppliers with currency and payment terms | Document defaults and due dates |
| Exchange rates for foreign currencies | Required on document date |
| Document sequences for current fiscal year | Number allocation on first save |

For document conversion rules after masters are ready, see <DocLink href="/docs/getting-started/document-relationships">Document relationships</DocLink>.
