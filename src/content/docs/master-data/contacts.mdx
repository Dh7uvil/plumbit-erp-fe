Contacts are people at customer organizations — used on sales documents for addressing and communication.

<AppLink href="/contacts">Open contacts</AppLink>

## What it controls

| Field | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `customer_id` | **Required** — parent customer |
| `name` | Contact person |
| `email` / `phone` | Communication details |
| `is_primary` | One primary contact per customer |
| `is_active` | Inactive contacts cannot be selected |

## Downstream fields

| Document | Field |
|---|---|
| Quotation, proforma, SO, SI | `contact_id` — must belong to document customer |

Primary contact auto-fills on some document conversions when not specified.

## Where used

Sales module only. Contacts are **customer-scoped** — there are no supplier contacts in the current model.

## Constraints

Unique index ensures at most one `is_primary = true` contact per customer.

## Example

Customer ABC has primary contact "John Smith". New quotations for ABC default to John; selecting another ABC contact is allowed but must match the header customer.
