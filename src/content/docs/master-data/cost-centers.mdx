Cost centers are accounting dimensions for tracking P&L and expenses by department, project, or business unit.

<AppLink href="/cost-centers">Open cost centers</AppLink>

## What it controls

| Field | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `code` | Unique identifier |
| `name` | Display label |
| `description` | Optional notes |
| `is_active` | Inactive cost centers cannot be selected |

## Downstream fields

| Record | Field |
|---|---|
| Journal entry | Header or line `cost_center_id` |
| Voucher lines | `cost_center_id` |
| Budget lines | `cost_center_id` |

## Where used

Manual journals, vouchers, budgets, and cost-center P&L reports. Commercial documents (SO, PO, invoices) do **not** carry cost center — allocation happens in accounting entries.

See <DocLink href="/docs/organization-and-access/organization-settings">Organization settings</DocLink> for the distinction between cost center (accounting) and branch (operational).

## Example

Journal entry debits travel expense and credits bank, both tagged to cost center "Marketing". P&L by cost center report shows the expense under Marketing only.
