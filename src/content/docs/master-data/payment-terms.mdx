Payment terms define how many days after invoice date payment is due.

<AppLink href="/payment-terms">Open payment terms</AppLink>

## What it controls

| Field | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `name` | Label (e.g. Net 30) |
| `days` | Due date offset (0–3650) |
| `description` | Optional notes |
| `is_active` | Inactive terms cannot be selected |

Seeded default: **Net 30** (30 days).

## Downstream fields

| Record | Behavior |
|---|---|
| Customer | `payment_terms_id` — default on sales docs |
| Quotation, proforma, SO, SI | Header payment terms; due date = document date + days |

## Where used

Sales documents and AR aging. Purchase documents can also reference payment terms for supplier due dates.

## Example

Customer with Net 30 terms receives an invoice dated 1 March — due date 31 March. Changing customer terms does not retroactively update posted invoices.
