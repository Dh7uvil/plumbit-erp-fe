Terms templates store reusable legal and commercial text copied onto quotations.

<AppLink href="/terms-templates">Open terms templates</AppLink>

## What it controls

| Field | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `name` | Template label |
| `body` | Full terms text (multi-line) |
| `is_default` | One default per tenant — pre-selected on new quotations |
| `is_active` | Inactive templates cannot be selected |

Seeded default: "Standard terms" with boilerplate about prices, currency, and validity.

## Downstream fields

| Document | Behavior |
|---|---|
| Quotation | Selecting `terms_template_id` copies `body` to document `terms_body` |

The copied text is editable on the quotation after selection — later template changes do not update existing documents.

## Where used

Quotations primarily. Other document types may carry free-text terms fields independently.

## Example

"Export terms" template includes incoterms and bank details. User selects it on a quotation; body copies to the document and can be tweaked for that deal without changing the master template.
