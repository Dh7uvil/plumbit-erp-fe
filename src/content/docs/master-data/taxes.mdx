Taxes define VAT rates and categories applied to products and document lines.

<AppLink href="/taxes">Open taxes</AppLink>

## What it controls

| Field | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `name` | Tax label (e.g. Standard VAT 5%) |
| `tax_category` | `STANDARD`, `ZERO_RATED`, `EXEMPT`, `OUT_OF_SCOPE` |
| `rate` | Percentage |
| `is_default` | Default tax for new products — one per tenant |
| `is_active` | Inactive taxes cannot be selected |

Seeded on tenant create: Standard VAT 5% (default), Zero Rated, Exempt, Out of Scope.

## Downstream fields

| Record | Field |
|---|---|
| Product | `tax_id` — default on lines |
| Document lines | Tax resolved from product or line override |
| Charge type | `default_tax_id` |
| VAT reports | Grouped by tax category |

## Where used

Sales and purchase documents, credit/debit notes, charge types on import invoices. Tax treatment on customers (`REGISTERED`, `EXPORT`, etc.) affects line eligibility — see <DocLink href="/docs/accounting/vat">VAT</DocLink>.

## Example

Product defaults to Standard VAT 5%. An export customer with `tax_treatment = EXPORT` may receive zero-rated lines on invoices regardless of product default, per document tax rules.
