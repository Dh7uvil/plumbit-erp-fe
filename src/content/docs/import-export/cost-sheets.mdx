Cost sheets plan and reconcile **landed unit costs** for import or export trades. They compare estimated charges and product values against actual posted purchase invoices.

<AppLink href="/cost-sheets">Open cost sheets</AppLink>

## Purpose

| Use | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Pre-landed planning** | Estimate freight, duty, and margin before goods arrive |
| **Post-landed reconciliation** | Pull actual amounts from posted expense PIs; compare to estimates |
| **Selling price target** | Optional target selling price and expected margin per line |

Cost sheets are **informational** — confirming or closing a sheet does not post stock or GL entries. Actual capitalization happens on <DocLink href="/docs/purchases/landed-costs">landed cost</DocLink> post.

## Types and status

| Sheet type | Typical link |
|---|---|
| **IMPORT** | Purchase order and/or import shipment |
| **EXPORT** | Customer, proforma invoice |
| **OTHER** | Ad-hoc analysis |

## Structure

**Product lines** — quantity, base rate, goods value, estimated and actual landed unit cost, optional target selling price.

**Charge lines** — charge type, estimated amount, actual amount (from linked PI line), variance, allocation basis, inventoriable flag.

**Totals** — goods value, inventoriable vs expensed charges (estimated/actual), weighted landed unit cost, FOB/CIF totals, reference selling total, sheet margin.

## Linking documents

A cost sheet can reference:

- Shipment (`shipment_id`)
- Purchase order (`purchase_order_id`)
- Supplier or customer
- Proforma invoice (export)
- Posted landed cost document (`landed_cost_id`) after capitalization

## Allocation method

Header `allocation_method` defaults charge spread across lines: **VALUE**, **WEIGHT**, **QUANTITY**, or **VOLUME**. Individual charge lines can override basis.
