International trade in Plumbit ERP covers **logistics tracking** (packages, shipments) and **landed cost planning** (cost sheets, charge types, landed costs). This is separate from CSV master-data import — see <DocLink href="/docs/master-data/importing-and-exporting-data">Importing and exporting data</DocLink>.

## Key principle

**Shipments and packages do not move stock or post to the GL.** Stock changes happen on posted goods receipts and delivery notes. Shipments are carrier and document tracking only — see <DocLink href="/docs/getting-started/implementation-notes">Implementation notes</DocLink>.
