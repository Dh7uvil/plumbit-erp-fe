UAE VAT zero-rated exports require **export evidence** — typically BL/AWB and customs exit documentation — linked to the export sale. Plumbit tracks compliance via attachments and an exceptions report.

<AppLink href="/reports/export-evidence-exceptions">Export evidence exceptions</AppLink>

## What counts as evidence

Attach documents on the **sales invoice**, **delivery note**, or **export shipment**:

| Category | Examples |
|---|---|
| **BL_DOCUMENT** | Bill of lading, air waybill |
| **CUSTOMS_DOC** | Customs exit proof, export declarations |

BL/AWB number and date can also be captured on the <DocLink href="/docs/import-export/shipments">shipment</DocLink> header (`bl_awb_number`, `bl_awb_date`).

## 90-day window

Posted zero-rated export invoices must have evidence within **90 days** (UAE VAT rule — enforced server-side as `window_days`).

The system sets `export_evidence_ok` on the sales invoice when evidence is satisfied. Posted export invoices missing proof appear on:

- <AppLink href="/reports/export-evidence-exceptions">Export evidence exceptions</AppLink> report
- Sales invoice list banner (exception count)
- Sales invoice detail warning when `is_export` and evidence is missing

Report columns: invoice, date, customer, grand total, **days elapsed**, **window** (90). Rows highlight **overdue** when days elapsed exceeds the window.

Filter by **as of** date. CSV export supported.
