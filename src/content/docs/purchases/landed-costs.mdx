A landed cost document spreads **posted expense purchase invoice lines** (freight, duty, clearing, etc.) across **posted goods receipt lines**, increasing FIFO **landed unit cost** on remaining stock.

## Charges and allocations

| Side | Source requirement | Content |
|---|---|---|
| **Charges** | Posted PI **expense** lines | Inventoriable charge types (`FREIGHT`, `CUSTOMS_DUTY`, etc.) |
| **Allocations** | Posted GRN **product** lines | Receipt lines receiving a share of each charge |

Both GRN and expense PI must be **posted**. See <DocLink href="/docs/getting-started/document-relationships">Document relationships</DocLink>.

## Allocation methods

Each charge spreads its amount across allocation lines proportionally:

| Method | Basis |
|---|---|
| **VALUE** | Line qty × landed unit cost (or qty × rate fallback) |
| **WEIGHT** | Line `net_weight` — required on product |
| **QUANTITY** | Line qty |
| **VOLUME** | Line `volume` — required on product |

Charge type default `allocation_basis` can override the header method per charge.

On-hand quantity is unchanged — only the **unit cost** on FIFO layers increases. Subsequent COGS and GRN-linked PI price variance use the updated landed cost.

## Cancellation rules (posted)

Cannot cancel when:

- Accounting period is locked
- A **later posted landed cost** touches the same GRN lines
