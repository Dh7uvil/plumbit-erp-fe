A purchase order (PO) is a **commitment to a supplier** for specific products and quantities. It authorizes purchasing but does not receive stock or create a payable.

## Line quantity tracking

Each line tracks:

- **Ordered** — `quantity`
- **Received** — `qty_received` (from posted GRNs)
- **Billed** — `qty_billed` (from posted PIs)
- **Returned** — `qty_returned`

Remaining to receive = `quantity − qty_received + qty_returned`. Header **receipt_status** and **billing_status** roll up separately.

<Callout type="implementation">
POs are **never auto-closed** when fully received or billed. Close manually when done.
</Callout>

## Partial receipt and billing

Multiple goods receipts and purchase invoices can reference the same PO while outstanding quantity remains. Over-receipt on GRN is controlled by org inbound settings — see <DocLink href="/docs/purchases/goods-receipts">Goods receipts</DocLink>.

## Cancellation rules

An **issued** PO cannot cancel if `receipt_status ≠ NOT_RECEIVED` or `billing_status ≠ NOT_INVOICED`.

## Downstream documents

From an **issued** PO you can create: goods receipt, purchase invoice, supplier payment (with PO link for advances). POs can also be generated from a sales order.
