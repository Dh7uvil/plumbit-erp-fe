A goods receipt (GRN) records physical receipt of stock into a warehouse. **Posting** increases on-hand, creates a FIFO cost layer, and posts **GRNI** — it does not create accounts payable.

## Creating a GRN

| Source | Source status | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Issued purchase order | `ISSUED` | Standard PO receipt; updates PO `qty_received` and reduces incoming |
| Posted bill | `POSTED` | bill-before-receipt flow; updates bill line `qty_received` |
| Standalone | — | No PO/bill link; qty not capped by order outstanding |

Movement type is **`PURCHASE`** for domestic suppliers and **`IMPORT`** when the supplier is export-treated or place of supply is outside UAE. See <DocLink href="/docs/inventory/how-stock-changes">How stock changes</DocLink>.

Unit cost on the FIFO layer = line rate × exchange rate (base currency). Only **FIFO** costing is supported.

## Line quantity tracking

Each product line tracks:

- **Received** — line `quantity`
- **Accepted / rejected / on hold** — from quality inspection (`qty_accepted`, `qty_rejected`, `qty_on_hold`)
- **Billed** — `qty_billed` (from posted bills)
- **Returned** — `qty_returned` (from posted purchase returns)

## Over-receipt

When linked to a PO, received qty is capped by PO line outstanding unless org inbound settings allow more:

| Setting | Effect |
|---|---|
| `allow_over_receipt` = false | Cannot exceed PO remaining qty |
| `allow_over_receipt` = true, no tolerance | Unlimited over PO line |
| `allow_over_receipt` = true + `over_receipt_tolerance_pct` | Allowed = remaining × (1 + pct ÷ 100) |

Bill-sourced lines are capped by bill line outstanding (`quantity − qty_received`) — PO over-receipt rules do not apply.

See <DocLink href="/docs/organization-and-access/organization-settings">Organization settings</DocLink>.

## QC hold

If the product has **requires QC** (default from org `qc_required_default`), the full line quantity goes on **QC hold** at post. On-hand increases but **available** does not until inspection is approved.

<Callout type="note">
QC hold details, dispositions, and scrap are documented in <DocLink href="/docs/inventory/quality-inspections-and-qc-hold">Quality inspections and QC hold</DocLink>.
</Callout>

## Partial receipt

Multiple GRNs can reference the same PO while outstanding quantity remains. Each posted GRN increments PO `qty_received` independently.

## Cancellation rules (posted)

A posted GRN **cannot be cancelled** when any of the following apply:

- Accounting period is locked (unless period override permission)
- Any GRN line has `qty_billed > 0`
- Linked PO has **any** billing on its lines — even if this GRN was not billed
- An **approved** quality inspection exists for the GRN
- A live (non-cancelled) **purchase return** references the GRN
- FIFO layers from the receipt are **not fully remaining** (partially consumed or revalued)
- Any line has inspection quantities recorded (`qty_accepted > 0` or `qty_rejected > 0`)

<Callout type="implementation">
A posted GRN **cannot be cancelled** if the linked PO has **any** billing, even when this GRN itself was never billed. See <DocLink href="/docs/getting-started/implementation-notes">Implementation notes</DocLink>.
</Callout>

## Downstream documents

From a **posted** GRN you can create: bill, purchase return, landed cost allocation target. Quality inspection is auto-created when QC is required.
