A purchase invoice (PI) is the supplier **bill**. **Posting** creates accounts payable and clears **GRNI** for received goods — it does **not** move stock (stock was received on the GRN).

## Bill types

| Type | Typical use | Stock link |
|---|---|---|
| **GOODS** | Domestic product purchases | GRN-linked or PO-linked product lines |
| **EXPENSE** | Freight, duty, clearing, insurance, other charges | Expense lines only — no stock |
| **IMPORT** | Import purchases | GRN-linked; RCM VAT defaults on |

Line types: **PRODUCT** (inventory/purchase account) or **EXPENSE** (expense account by category: `FREIGHT`, `CUSTOMS_DUTY`, `INSURANCE`, `CLEARING`, `INSPECTION`, `OTHER`).

Expense lines on posted **EXPENSE** or **IMPORT** bills can later feed **landed cost** capitalization onto GRN FIFO layers.

## Creating a PI

| Source | Source status | What gets billed |
|---|---|---|
| Posted goods receipt | `POSTED` | Unbilled GRN qty at GRN rate |
| Issued purchase order | `ISSUED` | Unbilled PO qty (no GRN required) |
| Standalone | — | Manual lines; no GRN/PO link |

Posted PI can also be the **source for a GRN** in the PI-first flow — see <DocLink href="/docs/getting-started/document-relationships">Document relationships</DocLink>.

## GRN-linked vs no-GRN posting

**GRN-linked product lines** — journal clears GRNI at the weighted FIFO **landed unit cost** from the receipt layers. The bill amount may differ:

- **Dr GRNI** — GRN unit cost × billed qty
- **Price variance** — difference between bill net and GRNI amount splits to:
  - **Dr Inventory** — revalues remaining FIFO layers to bill unit cost
  - **Dr Purchase price variance (PPV)** — consumed-qty portion

**No-GRN product lines** — **Dr Purchases** (product purchase account or default purchases account), **Cr AP**. No GRNI involved.

## Quantity headroom

Cannot bill more than source outstanding:

- GRN line: `quantity − qty_billed`
- PO line: `quantity − qty_billed`

Billing beyond headroom is blocked at post.

## Reverse charge (RCM) VAT

`is_reverse_charge` can be set explicitly. **IMPORT** bills default to reverse charge.

When RCM applies, line tax rates are forced to zero. The header computes `rcm_taxable_amount` and `rcm_tax_amount` at the standard VAT rate. GL posts **Dr VAT RCM input, Cr VAT RCM output** — not VAT input.

Non-RCM bills post **Dr VAT input**. See <DocLink href="/docs/accounting/vat">VAT</DocLink>.

## Auto-apply PO advances

After post, if the PI links to a PO, the system can automatically allocate unapplied **PO advance payments** to the invoice balance.

Gated by org setting `auto_apply_advances_on_invoice` (default **true**). Advances are applied FIFO by payment date. See <DocLink href="/docs/purchases/supplier-payments">Supplier payments</DocLink>.

## Cancellation rules (posted)

A posted PI **cannot be cancelled** when:

- Accounting period is locked
- `amount_paid > 0`, `amount_debited > 0`, or `amount_written_off > 0`
- A live **debit note** references the PI
- Live **supplier payment allocations** exist against the PI
- A **goods receipt** was created from this PI (PI-first flow)

Cancel reverses the journal and restores GRN FIFO layer costs to pre-bill values.

## Downstream documents

From a **posted** PI: supplier payment allocation, debit note, goods receipt (PI-first), landed cost charge source (expense lines).
