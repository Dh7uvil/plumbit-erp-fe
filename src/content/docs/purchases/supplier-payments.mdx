Supplier payments record money sent to suppliers. **Posting** credits the bank (or cash) account and debits **Advance to supplier**; **allocation** then clears open payables by debiting AP and crediting the advance.

<AppLink href="/supplier-payments">Open supplier payments</AppLink>

## Advance model

Plumbit uses an **advance-first** payment model — the same pattern as <DocLink href="/docs/sales/customer-payments">Customer payments</DocLink> on the sales side:

| Step | GL effect |
|---|---|
| **Post payment** | Cr bank/cash; Dr **Advance to supplier** for `amount_paid` |
| **Allocate to bill** | Dr **AP**; Cr **Advance to supplier** per allocation slice |

Unallocated balance is tracked as `amount_unapplied` on the payment header. Realized FX differences post to **FX gain/loss** on allocation.

<Callout type="note">
Customer receipts use **Advance from customer** with the mirror allocation pattern — see <DocLink href="/docs/sales/customer-payments">Customer payments</DocLink>.
</Callout>

## Allocation targets

Open items that can receive allocation:

- **Bill** — posted bills with `balance_due > 0`
- **Opening AP** — go-live supplier balances
- **Debit note** — netting only (via apply-debits-to-invoice, not direct payment allocation)

## PO advances

Link `purchase_order_id` on the payment to record an **advance against a PO** before bills exist. When a linked bill posts, org setting `auto_apply_advances_on_invoice` (default **true**) can FIFO-apply matching PO advances automatically.

Advance payments do not affect PO incoming qty or GRNI — they are purely financial until allocated.

## Cancellation rules (posted)

Cannot cancel when:

- A **refund** has been recorded against unapplied amounts
- Live **allocations** exist

## Related settings

| Setting | Effect |
|---|---|
| `auto_apply_advances_on_invoice` | Auto-allocate PO advances when bill posts |
| `vat_on_advances` | VAT treatment on advance post |
| `allow_negative_cash` | Cash account guard on post |

See <DocLink href="/docs/organization-and-access/organization-settings">Organization settings</DocLink>.
