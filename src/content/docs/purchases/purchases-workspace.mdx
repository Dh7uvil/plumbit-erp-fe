The **Purchases** workspace at <AppLink href="/purchases">/purchases</AppLink> is a combined list view of purchase orders and purchase invoices.

Use it to scan open commitments and bills in one place. Filters and column layout are remembered per screen.

Each document type has its own detail screens and documentation:

- <DocLink href="/docs/purchases/purchase-orders">Purchase orders</DocLink> — <AppLink href="/purchase-orders">/purchase-orders</AppLink>
- <DocLink href="/docs/purchases/purchase-invoices">Purchase invoices</DocLink> — <AppLink href="/purchase-invoices">/purchase-invoices</AppLink>

Goods receipts, supplier payments, debit notes, purchase returns, and landed costs are accessed from their dedicated menus — see <DocLink href="/docs/purchases/purchase-lifecycle">Purchase lifecycle</DocLink>.
