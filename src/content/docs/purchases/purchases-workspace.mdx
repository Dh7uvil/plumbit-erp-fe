The **Purchases** workspace at <AppLink href="/purchases">/purchases</AppLink> is the home for the full procure-to-pay cycle. There is no separate bills menu in the sidebar — orders and bills live here together.

## Tabs

| Tab | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Orders** | All purchase orders — filter by receipt and billing status |
| **Bills** | Posted and draft supplier bills |
| **To receive** | Issued POs with outstanding receipt qty |
| **To bill** | PO and GRN lines ready to convert into bills |

Use **To bill** or **Create bill** on an issued PO to record the supplier invoice. Filters and column layout are remembered per screen.
