Use **CRM activities** for scheduled tasks, calls, and meetings. This is the primary follow-up tool — there is no separate standalone tasks app.

<AppLink href="/activities">Open activities</AppLink>

## What activities cover

| Type | Typical use |
|---|---|
| **Task** | Follow up on quotation, chase payment |
| **Call** | Log phone conversation |
| **Meeting** | Schedule customer visit |

Link each activity to a lead, opportunity, customer, or contact. Set due date, priority, and owner.

## Follow-up views

| View | URL | Use |
|---|---|---|
| My open | `/activities?view=mine` | Your pending items |
| Overdue | `/activities?view=overdue` | Past due — linked from <DocLink href="/docs/getting-started/dashboard">Dashboard</DocLink> |
| This week | `/activities?view=week` | Upcoming week plan |

Mark **Completed** when done. Completed activities drop from open and overdue counts.

## CRM vs ERP documents

Activities do **not** replace document workflows:

- Payment collection → <DocLink href="/docs/sales/customer-payments">Customer payments</DocLink> + optional <DocLink href="/docs/collaboration/payment-reminders-and-emails">Payment reminders</DocLink>
- Order fulfillment → <DocLink href="/docs/sales/sales-orders">Sales orders</DocLink> and <DocLink href="/docs/sales/delivery-notes">Delivery notes</DocLink>

Activities track **human follow-up**, not stock or GL.
