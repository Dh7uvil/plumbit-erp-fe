Notes, attachments, and per-record history live on document and CRM detail screens — not as standalone modules.

## CRM notes

**Notes panel** on lead, opportunity, customer, and contact detail tabs. Requires `crm.note.*` permission.

Use for internal commentary that should not appear on printed documents. Notes are not exported to sales invoices or delivery notes.

## Attachments

**Entity attachments panel** on many detail screens:

| Document types | Examples |
|---|---|
| Sales | Sales orders, invoices, delivery notes, credit notes |
| Purchases | Purchase orders, goods receipts, bills, landed costs |
| Logistics | Shipments, packages |
| Payments | Customer and supplier payments |

| Category | Typical use |
|---|---|
| `BL_DOCUMENT` | Bill of lading, air waybill |
| `CUSTOMS_DOC` | Customs exit/import proof |
| `QC_PHOTO` | Quality inspection evidence |
| `POD` | Proof of delivery |
| `PACKING_LIST` | Carton contents |

Max file size: **25 MB** per upload. Attachments feed export evidence checks — see <DocLink href="/docs/import-export/export-evidence">Export evidence</DocLink>.

## Document history

Route pattern: `/history/{resource}/{id}`

| Document class | Title | Tabs |
|---|---|---|
| ERP transactions | Approvals and History | All activity, Edits, Attachments (+ approval timeline where applicable) |
| Master data | History | All activity, Edits, Attachments |

Available on quotations, orders, invoices, receipts, delivery notes, returns, packages, shipments, stock documents, journals, vouchers, payments, landed costs, and master records (customers, products, etc.).

Org-wide change log: <AppLink href="/audit-logs">Audit logs</AppLink> — see <DocLink href="/docs/organization-and-access/audit-logs-and-history">Audit logs and history</DocLink>.
