## Dunning rules

Each rule at <AppLink href="/dunning-rules">/dunning-rules</AppLink> defines:

| Field | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **days_offset** | Days from due date (negative = before due, positive = overdue) |
| **template_key** | Email template (due soon, overdue, etc.) |
| **is_active** | Include in automated scan |

Permissions: `accounting.dunning.read`, `accounting.dunning.manage`, `accounting.dunning.send`.

## Sending reminders

| Method | Behavior |
|---|---|
| **Scheduled scan** | Job evaluates active rules; one email per invoice + rule pair; skips if already sent |
| **Manual from invoice** | User triggers from posted sales invoice detail; picks rule or system matches by offset |

Reminder history appears on the invoice **Payment reminders** card.

## Email delivery

Requires tenant email configuration and customer contact email. Notification channel toggles at <DocLink href="/docs/getting-started/notifications">Notifications</DocLink> affect delivery preferences — there is no in-app reminder inbox.
