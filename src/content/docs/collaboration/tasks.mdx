Tasks are org-wide work items with issue types (Task, Bug, Story, Epic), parent/subtask hierarchy, assignees, due dates, labels, checklists, comments, and optional links to CRM or ERP records.

<AppLink href="/tasks">Open tasks</AppLink>

Use the board toggle on the tasks page for a kanban view grouped by status. Optional swimlanes add horizontal rows grouped by assignee, priority, or epic — drag cards across rows to reassign, reprioritize, or link to an epic.

Quick filters are independent toggles in the URL:

| Toggle | URL param | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| My open | `mine=1` | Tasks assigned to you |
| Overdue | `overdue=1` | Past due and not done |
| This week | `due_this_week=1` | Due in the current week |
| Show subtasks | `subtasks=1` | Include child tasks on the board (board view only) |
| Swimlanes | `swimlane=assignee` | Board rows by assignee (`priority` or `epic` also supported) |

Combine filters freely, for example `/tasks?mine=1&overdue=1&view=board&swimlane=assignee`.
