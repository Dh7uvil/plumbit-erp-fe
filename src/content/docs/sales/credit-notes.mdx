A credit note **reduces what a customer owes**. It posts to the general ledger only — no stock movement.

## GL account

Credit notes always debit the **Sales Returns** system account (`SALES_RETURNS`) for product line revenue — not the original product income account.

<Callout type="implementation">
This differs from some ERPs that credit the original revenue account. See <DocLink href="/docs/getting-started/implementation-notes">Implementation notes</DocLink>.
</Callout>

## Create from

| Source | Source must be |
|---|---|
| Sales invoice | POSTED |
| Sales return | POSTED |

Physical returns use <DocLink href="/docs/sales/sales-returns">Sales returns</DocLink> for stock; the credit note handles the commercial reduction.

<Example title="Partial credit against invoice">
Post CN for AED 500 linked to SI-1001 → Dr Sales returns 500, Cr AR 500. Invoice `amount_credited` increases; balance due drops.
</Example>
