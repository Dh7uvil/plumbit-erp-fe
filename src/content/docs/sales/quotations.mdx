A quotation is a **commercial offer** to a customer. It does not reserve stock or post to the general ledger.

**EXPIRED** is computed when `valid_until` has passed — the stored status remains `SENT` until revised or cancelled. Lists and detail views show the effective status as EXPIRED.

<Callout type="implementation">
When `quotation_requires_approval` is enabled, **send from draft is blocked**. Submit → approve → send. There are no amount-based approval thresholds — see <DocLink href="/docs/getting-started/implementation-notes">Implementation notes</DocLink>.
</Callout>

## Conversion paths

<Steps>
<Step title="Sales order">
From **ACCEPTED** or **PARTIALLY_CONVERTED**. Creates a confirmed-path sales order draft with line links back to the quotation.
</Step>
<Step title="Proforma invoice">
From **SENT** or **ACCEPTED**. Use for advance billing before fulfillment — see <DocLink href="/docs/sales/proforma-invoices">Proforma invoices</DocLink>.
</Step>
<Step title="Sales invoice">
From **ACCEPTED** or **PARTIALLY_CONVERTED**. Skips order and delivery when you bill directly from the quote.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Example title="Expiry">
A quotation sent on 1 Jan with `valid_until = 31 Jan` shows status **EXPIRED** from 1 Feb onward. Revise to draft to update terms and re-send.
</Example>
