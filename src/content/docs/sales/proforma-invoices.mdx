A proforma invoice is a **preliminary customer bill** used to request payment before final invoicing. It does not move stock or post revenue to the general ledger.

## Advances

Collect prepayment via <DocLink href="/docs/sales/customer-payments">Customer payments</DocLink> — the canonical advance page. Link the payment to the proforma or sales order when recording.

<Callout type="note">
When `auto_apply_advances_on_invoice` is enabled, posting a sales invoice linked to the proforma **automatically allocates** unapplied customer receipts to that invoice.
</Callout>

<Example title="Advance then invoice">
1. Confirm proforma for AED 10,000.
2. Post customer payment AED 10,000 → credit **Advance from customer**.
3. Convert and post sales invoice → advance allocates to AR; customer balance nets to zero.
</Example>
