A sales invoice is the **revenue and receivables** document. Posting creates AR, revenue, and output VAT. It does not issue stock — COGS is stamped from delivery data but posted only on the delivery note.

## COGS status

Each line and the header carry **cogs_status**:

| Value | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `NOT_APPLICABLE` | Service or non-stock line |
| `PENDING` | Tracked product with no posted delivery note for this qty |
| `PARTIAL` | Some qty delivered, some pending |
| `POSTED` | COGS already posted via delivery note |

<Callout type="implementation">
Sales invoices **without a delivery note** stamp COGS as **PENDING** indefinitely. GL COGS is recorded only when the delivery note posts — see <DocLink href="/docs/getting-started/implementation-notes">Implementation notes</DocLink>.
</Callout>

## Credit check on post

Credit control runs on **post** when:

- The invoice is **not linked to a sales order**, or
- `credit_limit_include_open_orders` is **off** (open SO value is excluded from exposure, so this invoice counts in full)

When the invoice is linked to an SO and open orders **are** included in exposure, the SO value already counted at confirm — only the incremental amount beyond that is evaluated. See <DocLink href="/docs/sales/credit-control">Credit control</DocLink>.

## Cancellation rules

A **posted** invoice cannot cancel if:

- `amount_paid > 0`
- `amount_credited > 0`
- `amount_written_off > 0`
- Dependent documents exist (e.g. posted delivery note created from invoice)

Use a **credit note** to reduce AR after payment or credit activity.

## Create from

Quotation, proforma, sales order, or posted delivery note(s) — see <DocLink href="/docs/getting-started/document-relationships">Document relationships</DocLink>.

<Example title="Invoice before delivery">
Post SI for 50 units with no DN → revenue and AR post immediately; cogs_status = PENDING. Post DN later → COGS hits the ledger on the DN, not the SI.
</Example>
