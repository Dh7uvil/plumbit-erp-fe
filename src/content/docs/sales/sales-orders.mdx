A sales order is a **customer commitment** for products and quantities. Confirming reserves available stock; it does not issue stock or create receivables until downstream documents post.

## Fulfillment and billing roll-ups

**fulfillment_status** and **billing_status** are independent header fields:

| Field | Values | Driven by |
|---|---|---|
| **fulfillment_status** | NOT_DELIVERED → PARTIALLY_DELIVERED → DELIVERED | Posted delivery note quantities |
| **billing_status** | NOT_INVOICED → PARTIALLY_INVOICED → INVOICED | Posted sales invoice quantities |

Each line tracks ordered, delivered, returned, invoiced, and reserved quantities separately.

## Stock reservation

On **confirm**, tracked lines call `reserve_locked`. Reservation target = `quantity − qty_delivered − qty_returned`.

<Callout type="note">
Shortfalls **never block confirm**. If available qty is insufficient, the system reserves what exists and reports the shortfall. See <DocLink href="/docs/inventory/stock-concepts">Stock concepts</DocLink> and <DocLink href="/docs/inventory/how-stock-changes">How stock changes</DocLink>.
</Callout>

<Callout type="implementation">
Outstanding delivery qty on the SO **ignores returns**; the reservation formula **subtracts returns**. See <DocLink href="/docs/getting-started/implementation-notes">Implementation notes</DocLink>.
</Callout>

## Credit check

Credit control runs on **confirm** when `credit_limit_policy` is WARN or BLOCK. Users with `CREDIT_CONTROL_OVERRIDE` can override BLOCK with a reason — see <DocLink href="/docs/sales/credit-control">Credit control</DocLink>.

## Cancellation rules

A **confirmed** order cannot cancel once `fulfillment_status ≠ NOT_DELIVERED` or `billing_status ≠ NOT_INVOICED`.

## Downstream documents

From a **confirmed** sales order you can create:

- **Delivery note** — issues stock on post
- **Sales invoice** — bills without delivery when needed
- **Purchase order** — plan or create POs for buy-to-order lines

<Example title="Partial fulfillment">
SO for 100 units → DN posted for 40 → fulfillment_status PARTIALLY_DELIVERED, reservation target drops to 60 undelivered.
</Example>
