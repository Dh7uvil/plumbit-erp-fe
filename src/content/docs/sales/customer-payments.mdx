## Advances

On **post**, the full receipt (net of bank charges) credits **Advance from customer** until you allocate it.

<Callout type="note">
When `vat_on_advances` is enabled, standard-rated advances split into net advance and VAT output on post. When off, the entire receipt goes to advance with no VAT line.
</Callout>

<Steps>
<Step title="Record the receipt">
Create payment, select customer and bank account, enter amount. Optionally link a sales order or proforma for traceability.
</Step>
<Step title="Post">
Creates the bank debit and advance credit (+ VAT if configured).
</Step>
<Step title="Allocate">
Apply unapplied balance to open sales invoices or credit notes — Dr Advance, Cr AR.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Cheques and PDC

Post-dated cheques are tracked in a **separate module** — not on the customer payment screen. See <DocLink href="/docs/accounting/cheques-and-pdc">Cheques and PDC</DocLink>.

<Example title="Advance before invoice">
Customer pays AED 5,000 against a confirmed proforma → post payment → Advance from customer AED 5,000. Post sales invoice later → auto-apply clears AR using the advance balance.
</Example>
