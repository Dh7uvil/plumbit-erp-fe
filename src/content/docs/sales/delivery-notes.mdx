A delivery note records **physical shipment** from a warehouse. Posting is the stock-out and COGS event for domestic and export sales.

## Stock movement type

| Tax treatment | Movement type |
|---|---|
| Domestic sale | `SALE` |
| Export | `EXPORT` |

Post sequence for SO-linked lines: **release reservation** (up to delivery qty) → **issue on FIFO** → update sales order delivery quantities.

<Callout type="note">
Service and non-tracked products appear on the document but skip stock operations — see <DocLink href="/docs/inventory/stock-concepts">Stock concepts</DocLink>.
</Callout>

<Callout type="implementation">
Cancelling a posted delivery note **reverses stock and GL** but **does not re-reserve** the sales order. Outstanding delivery qty increases; reserved qty is unchanged by the cancel.
</Callout>

## Downstream

From a **posted** delivery note:

- **Sales invoice** — bill delivered qty (if not already invoiced)
- **Sales return** — must reference this posted DN

<Example title="Export delivery">
Export DN with `tax_treatment = EXPORT` posts an `EXPORT` movement and COGS at FIFO cost — same GL pattern as domestic (Dr COGS, Cr Inventory).
</Example>
