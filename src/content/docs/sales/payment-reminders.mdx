Payment reminders (dunning) send **email notices** for overdue or upcoming sales invoices. No general-ledger entries are created.

## Dunning rules

Each rule defines:

| Field | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **days_offset** | Days from invoice due date (negative = before due, positive = overdue) |
| **template_key** | Email template (e.g. payment due soon, payment overdue) |
| **is_active** | Include in automated scan |

Rules are maintained at <AppLink href="/dunning-rules">Dunning rules</AppLink>.

## Scan vs manual send

  rows={[

**Automated scan** evaluates all active rules for the tenant as of a calendar date. For each rule it finds open invoices whose due date matches `as_of − days_offset`, then enqueues a reminder if none was already logged for that invoice and rule.

**Manual send** from a posted invoice resolves the applicable rule (explicit rule ID or best match by days from due date) and sends immediately.

<Callout type="note">
Invoices without a **due date** cannot receive automated or manual reminders.
</Callout>

<Example title="7-day overdue rule">
Rule: `days_offset = 7`, template PAYMENT_OVERDUE. Scan on 15 Feb targets invoices with due date 8 Feb that still have balance due.
</Example>

## What is not affected

- No AR, revenue, or tax postings
- No change to invoice payment status
- Reminder history is stored in dunning logs on the invoice
