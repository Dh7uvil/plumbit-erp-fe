Stock replenishment in Plumbit ERP is **rule-based**, not AI. Set reorder levels per product per warehouse; the purchase suggestions report lists items at or below threshold.

<AppLink href="/reports/purchase-suggestions">Purchase suggestions</AppLink>

## Reorder levels

Set on warehouse stock balance screens:

| Screen | Route |
|---|---|
| Stock list | <AppLink href="/stock">/stock</AppLink> |
| Product warehouse detail | <AppLink href="/stock">/stock/{productId}</AppLink> |

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Reorder level** | Minimum available qty before replenishment is suggested |
| **Reorder qty** | Suggested purchase quantity when at or below level |

Available qty = on hand − reserved − QC hold. See <DocLink href="/docs/inventory/reorder-levels">Reorder levels</DocLink> and <DocLink href="/docs/inventory/stock-concepts">Stock concepts</DocLink>.

## Purchase suggestions report

Route: <AppLink href="/reports/purchase-suggestions">/reports/purchase-suggestions</AppLink>

Lists products where **available ≤ reorder level** with suggested reorder quantity. Permission: `reports.report.inventory`.

The report does **not** create purchase orders — review suggestions, adjust for lead times and open POs, then create POs manually.

## What this is not

- No machine-learning demand forecast
- No automatic PO generation
- No supplier selection logic

For AI capabilities, see <DocLink href="/docs/planning-and-ai/ai-features">AI features</DocLink>.
