AI assistant, natural-language prompting, and machine-learning demand forecasting are **not available** in Plumbit ERP.

## What exists today

| Feature | Type | Page |
|---|---|---|
| Purchase suggestions | Rule-based (reorder level) | <DocLink href="/docs/planning-and-ai/stock-recommendations">Stock recommendations</DocLink> |
| Credit control exposure | Deterministic formula | <DocLink href="/docs/sales/credit-control">Credit control</DocLink> |
| Export evidence check | Rule-based (90-day window) | <DocLink href="/docs/import-export/export-evidence">Export evidence</DocLink> |

These use configured thresholds and posted data — they do not learn from history or accept conversational input.

## Not available

- In-app AI chat or copilot
- Natural-language report queries
- Automated demand or price forecasting
- Document OCR or intelligent data entry

<Callout type="important">
Always verify system-generated numbers against your own knowledge of stock, lead times, and customer commitments before acting.
</Callout>
