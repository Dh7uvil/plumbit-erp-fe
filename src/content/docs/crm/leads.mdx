Leads are potential customers before they exist in the party master. Qualify, assign, and **convert** to a customer, contact, and optional opportunity.

Leads in **CONVERTED** or **LOST** status cannot be edited.

## Convert flow

Requires `crm.lead.convert` permission. From lead detail, open **Convert**:

| Step | Options |
|---|---|
| **Customer** | Create new (name + tax treatment) or link existing customer |
| **Contact** | Name, email, phone (primary contact) |
| **Opportunity** | Optional — checkbox + name (defaults from company/contact) |

On success, the system creates the customer and contact, optionally an opportunity, and sets lead status to **CONVERTED**. If an opportunity was created, navigation goes to that opportunity.

Converted leads retain a link to the created customer for traceability.

## Fields

Typical lead data: company name, contact name, email, phone, lead source, assigned owner, status, lost reason (when LOST), notes.

Filter the list by status. Sort by lead number, company, status, or created date.
