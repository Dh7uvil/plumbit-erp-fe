Lead sources and lost reasons are small master-data lists that classify **where leads come from** and **why deals were lost**.

<AppLink href="/lead-sources">Lead sources</AppLink> · <AppLink href="/lost-reasons">Lost reasons</AppLink>

## Lead sources

Assign a source when creating or editing a lead (e.g. Website, Referral, Trade show). Sources are tenant-defined at <AppLink href="/lead-sources">Lead sources</AppLink>.

Used in:

- Lead list filters and detail
- **Lead conversion** report — measures conversion rate by source

## Lost reasons

Required when marking a lead or opportunity **LOST**. Examples: Price, No budget, Chose competitor, Timing.

Maintain at <AppLink href="/lost-reasons">Lost reasons</AppLink>. Used in:

- Lead and opportunity close-lost dialogs
- **Win / loss** report — breakdown of lost deals by reason

## Setup

Both are simple name + active flag records. Deactivate rather than delete if historical records reference the value.
