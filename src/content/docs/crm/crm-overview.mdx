CRM manages the pipeline **before and alongside** sales documents — leads, opportunities, contacts, activities, and campaigns. CRM does not replace AR or inventory; it feeds into quotations and sales orders manually.

See <DocLink href="/docs/getting-started/system-overview">System overview</DocLink> for module relationships.
