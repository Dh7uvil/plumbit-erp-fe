Activities are tasks, calls, and meetings linked to CRM records. They drive follow-up discipline and appear on the dashboard when overdue.

<AppLink href="/activities">Open activities</AppLink>

## Types and status

| Type | Use |
|---|---|
| **Task** | To-do with due date |
| **Call** | Phone follow-up |
| **Meeting** | Scheduled meeting |

## Related entities

An activity can link to a **lead**, **opportunity**, **customer**, or **contact**. Subject, due date, priority, owner, and notes are editable from list dialogs.

## List views

| View (`view`) | Shows |
|---|---|
| **All** | Every activity you can read |
| **My open** (`mine`) | Your open activities |
| **Overdue** (`overdue`) | Past due and still open — linked from <DocLink href="/docs/getting-started/dashboard">Dashboard</DocLink> |
| **This week** (`week`) | Due within the current week |

Additional filters: status, activity type. Sort by due date, subject, status, priority, created.

## Permissions

Requires `crm.activity.read` to view, `crm.activity.manage` to create/edit. Complete and delete actions check write permission on the activity.
