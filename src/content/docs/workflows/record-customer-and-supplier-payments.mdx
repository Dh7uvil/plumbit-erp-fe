## Customer payment

<Steps>
<Step title="Create payment">
<AppLink href="/customer-payments">Open customer payments</AppLink> — select customer, bank account, amount. Optionally link sales order or proforma for traceability (advance before invoice).

See <DocLink href="/docs/sales/customer-payments">Customer payments</DocLink>.
</Step>

<Step title="Post">
**Post** — full receipt credits **Advance from customer** (plus output VAT on advance when `vat_on_advances` is enabled).

<StateChangeTable
  rows={[
    { action: "Post customer payment", stock: "No change", reserved: "No change", gl: "Dr Bank; Cr Advance from customer", tax: "Optional VAT on advance", documents: "amount_unapplied tracked" },
  ]}
/>
</Step>

<Step title="Allocate to invoices">
Apply unapplied balance to open sales invoices or credit notes — Dr Advance from customer, Cr AR.

When `auto_apply_advances_on_invoice` is on, posting a linked SI can FIFO-apply existing advances automatically.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Supplier payment

Same pattern on the payables side:

<Steps>
<Step title="Create payment">
<AppLink href="/supplier-payments">Open supplier payments</AppLink> — select supplier, bank/cash account, amount. Link `purchase_order_id` for PO advance before bills exist.
</Step>

<Step title="Post">
**Post** — credits bank; debits **Advance to supplier**.

<StateChangeTable
  rows={[
    { action: "Post supplier payment", stock: "No change", reserved: "No change", gl: "Cr bank; Dr advance to supplier", tax: "Optional VAT on advance", documents: "amount_unapplied tracked" },
  ]}
/>
</Step>

<Step title="Allocate to bills">
Apply to posted purchase invoices or opening AP items — Dr AP, Cr Advance to supplier. Realized FX differences post on allocation.

See <DocLink href="/docs/purchases/supplier-payments">Supplier payments</DocLink>.
</Step>
</Steps>
