## Option A: Opening balances wizard (go-live)

Use during initial setup — part of <DocLink href="/docs/workflows/go-live-setup">Go-live setup</DocLink>.

<Steps>
<Step title="Open wizard">
<AppLink href="/opening-balances">Open opening balances</AppLink> — add stock lines: product, warehouse, qty, unit cost.
</Step>

<Step title="Match inventory GL">
Ensure total FIFO value (qty × unit cost across all stock lines) equals the **inventory GL line** on the same opening journal.
</Step>

<Step title="Commit">
Commit posts OPENING_STOCK movements and sets `books_start_date` + hard lock.

## Option B: Stock adjustment (after go-live)

Use when adding or correcting opening qty **after** the tenant is live.

<Steps>
<Step title="Create adjustment">
<AppLink href="/stock-adjustments">Open stock adjustments</AppLink> — reason **OPENING_STOCK**. Enter product, warehouse, qty delta (or COUNT with `qty_counted`), and **unit cost** for FIFO layer creation.
</Step>

<Step title="Post">
Post creates OPENING_STOCK movement and posts Inventory ↔ Inventory adjustment GL. Does **not** change `books_start_date`.

|---|---|---|---|

| Adjustment (Opening stock) | Post go-live | No | Per-adjustment entry |
