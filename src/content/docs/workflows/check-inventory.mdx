<Step title="Open stock list">
<AppLink href="/stock">Open stock</AppLink> — view on-hand, reserved, incoming, and available qty by product and warehouse. Filter by warehouse, category, or below reorder level.

From a product row, open **movements** or navigate to <AppLink href="/stock-movements">stock movements</AppLink> filtered by SKU to audit PURCHASE, SALE, TRANSFER, and adjustment history.
</Step>
