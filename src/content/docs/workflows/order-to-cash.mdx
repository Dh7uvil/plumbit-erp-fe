<Prerequisites>
- Customer, products, and warehouse exist
- Permissions for quotations, sales orders, delivery notes, invoices, and payments
</Prerequisites>

## Standard path

<Steps>
<Step title="Create and confirm sales order">
Convert a quotation or create a sales order directly. **Confirm** the order — stock **reserves** for tracked lines.

  rows={[

  ]}
/>

Review **reservation shortfalls** on the order detail if confirm could not reserve the full quantity — see <DocLink href="/docs/inventory/negative-stock-and-shortfalls">Negative stock and shortfalls</DocLink>.
</Step>

<Step title="Deliver goods">
Create a <DocLink href="/docs/sales/delivery-notes">delivery note</DocLink> from the confirmed SO. **Post** the DN.

<StateChangeTable
  rows={[
    { action: "Post DN", stock: "On hand − (FIFO issue)", reserved: "Release then issue", gl: "Dr COGS, Cr Inventory", tax: "None", documents: "SO qty_delivered +; SALE or EXPORT movement" },
  ]}
/>

**Partial delivery** — post multiple DNs until each SO line is fully delivered. Each DN updates `qty_delivered` independently.

**Shortfall at post** — if `allow_negative_stock` is off and available qty is insufficient, DN post is **blocked** (unlike SO confirm, which never blocks).
</Step>

<Step title="Invoice customer">
Create a <DocLink href="/docs/sales/sales-invoices">sales invoice</DocLink> from posted DN(s) or the SO. **Post** the invoice.

/>
</Step>

<Step title="Collect payment">
Create a <DocLink href="/docs/sales/customer-payments">customer payment</DocLink>, **post**, and **allocate** to the open invoice — see <DocLink href="/docs/workflows/record-customer-and-supplier-payments">Record payments</DocLink>.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Variant: direct invoice (invoice before delivery)

Skip DN at invoice time when billing precedes shipment.

<Steps>
<Step title="Post sales invoice without DN">
Post SI from quotation or SO. Revenue and AR post immediately; `cogs_status = PENDING` on tracked lines.

<Step title="Deliver later (optional)">
Create DN from posted SI or SO. Post DN — COGS hits the ledger here, not on the SI.

</Step>

## Credit block on confirm or post

Credit control evaluates customer exposure against their limit on **SO confirm** and **SI post** (depending on linkage and org settings).

| Policy | Behavior |
|---|---|
| **OFF** | No enforcement |
| **WARN** | Banner; action proceeds |
| **BLOCK** | Blocked unless user has override permission + reason |

See <DocLink href="/docs/sales/credit-control">Credit control</DocLink>. Override requires `CREDIT_CONTROL_OVERRIDE`.

## Exception: return → credit note

When goods come back after delivery:

<Steps>
<Step title="Post sales return from DN">
Must reference a **posted** delivery note. Choose line disposition (RESTOCK, QC_HOLD, SCRAP).

Follow <DocLink href="/docs/workflows/handle-a-customer-return">Handle a customer return</DocLink>.
</Step>
<Step title="Post credit note">
Create CN from the posted return or source invoice. Post — AR reduced; no stock movement on CN.

## Exception: write-off

When an invoice balance is uncollectible, use **Write off** on a posted sales invoice (requires write-off permission). This clears remaining AR to bad debt expense — see <DocLink href="/docs/accounting/write-offs">Write-offs</DocLink>.

A posted SI with write-offs applied **cannot be cancelled**; use credit notes for commercial adjustments instead.

## Cancel guards

| Document | Cannot cancel when |
|---|---|
| **Posted DN** | Period locked; dependent SI or sales return exists |
| **Posted SI** | `amount_paid > 0`; `amount_credited > 0`; `amount_written_off > 0`; dependent posted DN |
| **Posted sales return** | Period locked; allocated credit note |
| **Posted CN** | Allocated to invoices |
