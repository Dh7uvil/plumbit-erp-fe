Review open receivables and payables, identify overdue items, and follow up before month-end close.

<Steps>
<Step title="Run AR aging">
<AppLink href="/reports/ar-aging">Open AR aging</AppLink> — bucket open sales invoices and opening AR by age (current, 30, 60, 90+ days). Filter by customer, branch, or salesperson.

See <DocLink href="/docs/accounting/accounts-receivable">Accounts receivable</DocLink>.
</Step>

<Step title="Run AP aging">
<AppLink href="/reports/ap-aging">Open AP aging</AppLink> — same buckets for bills and opening AP by supplier.

See <DocLink href="/docs/accounting/accounts-payable">Accounts payable</DocLink>.
</Step>

<Step title="Drill to statements">
Run <AppLink href="/reports/customer-statement">customer statement</AppLink> or <AppLink href="/reports/supplier-statement">supplier statement</AppLink> for a single party — shows invoices, payments, credits/debits, and running balance.
</Step>

<Step title="Follow up">
- Overdue AR → <DocLink href="/docs/sales/payment-reminders">Payment reminders</DocLink> or collect via <DocLink href="/docs/workflows/record-customer-and-supplier-payments">Record payments</DocLink>
- Disputed AP → <DocLink href="/docs/purchases/debit-notes">Debit notes</DocLink> or supplier return workflow
- Uncollectible AR → <DocLink href="/docs/accounting/write-offs">Write-offs</DocLink>
</Step>
</Steps>
